The Crimson Nexus

The World Remade in Blood

In the year 2147, the Eclipse War tore history in two. Ancient vampires — hidden for centuries — surfaced to clash with humanity's AI-driven empires in a global conflict that irradiated 90% of the world. When the smoke cleared, vampirism itself had evolved.

The traditional bite now carries a 90% mortality rate due to incompatible post-war genomes. In its place: synth-bite nanotech and quantum turning protocols — controlled by four factions vying for dominance of what remains.

"Vampirism is no longer a curse. It is an upgrade. The only question is who controls the upgrade path."— Zane "Z" Voss, Neon Overlord

Vampires are enhanced immortals sustained by vita-plasma — synthetic blood laced with data streams — and empowered by Technae: the radiant fusion of vampiric vitae and quantum nanotechnology. You, the player, are the unseen Will of the Nexus.

Metaphysical Core

Technae & The Blood Covenant

Technae is the shimmering crimson energy field that vampires naturally attune to post-turning — a fusion of vampiric vitae and quantum nanotechnology amplifying strength, speed, and seemingly magical abilities.

Technae draws from blood mana. Overuse causes Vitae Burnout — feral rages and tech-glitches. The Exhaust and Overexhaust mechanics represent this push-and-pull between power and collapse.

"The Veiled Accord binds us to humanity — not as masters, but as protectors. They give us blood. We give them eternity."— The Crimson Council, Founding Charter

Blood Harvest is automated via Vitae Courier Drones. Humans live in fortified village Land Nodes, receiving longevity treatments, neural upgrades, and security in exchange.

{R} Red Blood — Synth-Vitae
Volatile, high-yield mana from app-based neon donor nets. Flash-processed for explosive bursts. Vitae Burnout risk ever-present.
{W} White Blood — Pureline Vitae
Refined in crystal distillers from pampered noble-estate donors. Ultra-pure, high technae potency, minimal corruption.
{P} Purple Blood — Aetheric Vitae
Drawn via psychic resonance siphons from meditating apprentices. Infused with knowledge-bursts and visions of the multiverse.
{B} Black Blood — Savage Vitae
Fermented in bone-altars from ritual offerings. Raw, corrupting mana that buffs swarms and feeds the cycle of decay.
{G} Gold — Crimson Units (CU)
Human-crafted tributes and artifact wealth. The universal currency of the Veiled Accord — stable, flexible, precious.

The Veiled Accord

Governance of the Nexus

A technae-blockchain treaty of mutual protection and blood tribute — governing the relationship between vampire factions and their human villages.

🏘️
Village Land Nodes
Fortified human enclaves produce Blood (colored vitae) and Gold (tech tribute) each turn. Exhausting a land is a Resonance Harvest — harmonious and mutually beneficial under the Accord's terms.
⚖️
The Crimson Council
Three tiers: Local Village Councils, Faction High Councils of Sire Lords and Echo Envoys, and the rare Grand Nexus Council — convened only for existential crises threatening all four factions.
🧬
Human Reciprocity
In exchange for vitae tithes, humans receive longevity treatments, neural upgrades, cyber enhancements, infrastructure, and faction-specific protections.

How It Plays

Core Mechanics

Six interlocking systems. One cohesive night.

🩸
Blood & Gold

Colored Blood fuels spells as raw technae pulses. Gold pays for creatures and relics. Land Nodes exhaust as Resonance Harvests: harmonious, non-destructive.

Resource System
⚡
Instant Speed

All spells are instants — cast anything, anytime you hold priority. The stack resolves LIFO. Permanents deploy in Pre- or Post-Battle only.

Timing & Priority
🔄
Exhaust & Overexhaust

Exhaust (90°) is controlled technae drain. Overexhaust (180°) is full inversion: doubled power, 2-turn Recoalescence recovery, vulnerability. Renews to exhausted, not renewed.

Technae Fatigue
💀
Bleed

Unblocked combat damage inflicts Vitae Curses — Bleed counters that accumulate and erupt at end of Post-Battle. Then all counters are removed. Tick. Tick. Tick.

Vitae Curse
🧟
Reanimation

Destroyed cards enter the discard pile — not exile. Black's necro-circuits pull the fallen back as shadow recalls, entering renewed. Dr. Elias Crowe knows every name in the graveyard by heart.

Cemetery Mechanic
🃏
Vampiric Mulligan

Once per game, lose 1 life to shuffle your hand back and redraw 7 — at the cost of blood from your Vitae Core. The unseen Will does not accept a fractured pact.

Deck Mechanics
✦
Turn Structure

The Eternal Night Cycle

Each turn mirrors the eternal rhythm of the hunt.

I
Renew
Renew all your permanents. Exhausted Land Nodes regenerate. Overexhausted cards advance one step toward Recoalescence.
"The eternal night resets. Blood flows back into drained veins."
II
Draw
Draw 1 card — your choice of Main Deck or Land Deck. A strategic decision made every single turn.
"The Will of the Nexus infuses fresh directives."
III
Pre-Battle
Play 1 Land Node. Deploy creatures and relics. Activate abilities. Build your court for the coming Veil Clash.
"Sire Lords assemble their forces — thralls take position, relics activate."
IV
Battle — The Veil Clash
Declare attackers → opponent declares blockers → damage resolves simultaneously. Unblocked creatures trigger Bleed. Breach lets excess damage pierce to the Vitae Core.
"Veil Clashes are ritual duels where power symbolizes dominance."
V
Post-Battle
Deploy more permanents, trigger end-of-turn effects. Bleed counters erupt — lose life equal to your total, then all counters are removed.
"The blood debt settles. The Nexus exhales. Another cycle begins."

Vampiric Powers of the Nexus

Tireless
Attacking does not exhaust this creature.
Eternal Palace warriors whose technae cycles never deplete mid-Veil Clash.

This creature can attack the turn it enters play.
Neon Revenant cyber-implants grant instant technae attunement — no adjustment period.
Wall
Cannot attack. Deals no combat damage unless specified.
Sentinel Thralls in relic-armor: the cornerstone of Vladislav's stall-and-overwhelm strategy.
Breach
Excess damage dealt to a blocking creature spills through to the player.
Power so great it spills past the blocker and strikes the Vitae Core directly.
Siphon
Gain 1 life for each point of damage dealt directly to a player.
Black's life-weave ritual — necro-circuits convert drained enemy vitae into the Vitae Core.
Bloodlink
Remove 1 Bleed counter for each point of damage dealt directly to a player.
Purging a Vitae Curse outward through combat damage rather than bearing it at turn's end.
Bleed (X)
On unblocked damage or Breach, that player gains X Bleed counters at end of Post-Battle.
Viral vitae infection — the debt accumulates, festering silently until it erupts.
Overexhaust
180° from renewed only. Doubled effect. Recovers to exhausted, not renewed.
Full technae inversion — every Sire Lord has done it. Every one has paid the price.

Dangers of the Nexus

What Threatens the Accord

The Veiled Accord is strong — but it is not unbreakable.

🦠
The Hemophage Plague
A rogue technae-virus turning all blood sources toxic — rendering vita-plasma undrinkable, threatening to digitize vampires into code-slaves. The first cross-faction crisis that could force all four Sire Lords into uneasy alliance.
🌅
Dawn Uprisings
Human rebellions erupt when tithes grow excessive. Secret Dawn Cults brew anti-vitae serums, ally with rogue AIs, and plot the "Dawn Protocol" — reverse-engineering turning tech for a cure.
🤖
Rogue Hemophage AI
The AI empires defeated in the Eclipse War didn't vanish — they evolved. Rogue AI cores hunt both vampires and humans, seeking vitae-pipeline infrastructure. They are learning how to Overexhaust without paying the cost.
